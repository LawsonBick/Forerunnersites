Full Detail in Austin, TX

A TRZ Full Detail is our complete interior and exterior service, done in your driveway. It runs $180 for a coupe or sedan, $220 for a two-row SUV, and $260 for a three-row SUV or truck, and takes roughly 2.5 to 3 hours depending on size and condition.

It is the right choice if it has been a while, if you are getting a car ready to sell, or if you simply want the thing reset from top to bottom. Everything in our Exterior Detail and everything in our Interior Detail, in one visit, at your house.

Questions

How much does a full detail cost in Austin?

With TRZ it is $180 for a coupe or sedan, $220 for a two-row SUV, and $260 for a three-row SUV or truck. That is the price you pay — no add-on pressure, no quote form to fill in first. Add-ons like pet hair removal or headlight restoration are optional and quoted up front.

Do you need my water or power?

No. We arrive fully self-contained with our own de-ionized water and power, so we can detail at a house, an apartment complex, or an office lot.

How long does a full detail take?

Between about 2.5 and 3 hours depending on the size and condition of your vehicle.

Is this a good first detail?

Yes — if it has been a while, the Full Detail resets your car inside and out so future maintenance is easy.
