Interior and exterior window cleaning

Choose the sides of the glass you want cleaned, including patio doors and larger architectural panes. Tell us about upper floors, furniture near interior windows, and any areas that are difficult to reach. Your estimate defines the agreed work.

What affects your window-cleaning estimate?

Pane count and size, glass condition, access, and interior or exterior scope affect the quote. Mention screens, tracks, sprinkler spotting, and construction residue separately so Apex can assess the work and explain any additional cost.

Planning window washing around your home

Share your city or ZIP code and the type of windows you have. Before the visit, agree on gate access, pets, and furniture that needs moving. If you want patio or driveway cleaning too, include it in the same inquiry so the sequence can be discussed.
